# Architecture and proof obligations

`model.py` validates the protocol and builds immutable values; `engine.py` evaluates lifecycle/conflicts/Boolean support and plans conditional repairs; `events.py` creates new validated snapshots; `cli.py` manages local JSON artifacts; `benchmark.py` implements explicit policy baselines. No network connector or scheduler is hidden behind the gate.

For V source versions, F fact assertions, E expression nodes, C claims, and A answers, validation/evaluation are linear apart from deterministic sorting, hashing and source-history sorting. The topological claim pass uses Kahn's algorithm, so long claim chains do not consume Python recursion. Expressions have a depth limit. Every fact-bearing claim observes all current fresh assertions of its key: this accounts for new conflicts outside its citation cone.

Evidence validity is the conjunction of observed, current, unrevoked and unexpired. Claim validity is its monotone Boolean support expression AND absence of structured fact blockers. Answer validity is its Boolean claim expression. A compare pass evaluates snapshots and reports **actual VALID → BLOCKED transitions**; a raw transitive source-dependency cone would unnecessarily invalidate alternatives. This implementation evaluates the full manifest, not a sublinear incremental index, because new fact conflicts can affect uncited answers. Definition changes and removed answers are reported separately from validity transitions.

The repair planner associates fresh leaves with the empty task set, stale leaves with their version-specific obligation, and fact blockers with a key-specific resolution obligation. OR takes a union of alternatives; AND takes pairwise set unions. After every step, strict supersets are eliminated. By induction over the acyclic expression graph, the resulting antichain is exactly the family of subset-minimal satisfying obligation sets under that conditional monotone model. Cardinality sorting gives a minimum-count choice without losing larger incomparable alternatives. Local greedy choice is incorrect when branches share tasks.

The conditional model assumes completing an obligation removes its blocker. Real execution can change facts and introduce blockers, so the library never declares a plan an automatic repair certificate. Distinct pinned revisions of one source can require different claim-review obligations; the task objective counts obligations, not HTTP requests. Witness planning uses fresh source-version leaves and no hypothetical repairs.

Frontiers may be exponential: AND of n disjoint two-way alternatives has 2^n witnesses. The explicit `max_work` budget bounds candidate enumeration; exhaustion raises `PlanningLimitError` and CLI exit 3 without a partial answer. Antichain subset comparisons and task-set unions still have nonconstant cost. Use process time/memory limits when ingesting untrusted graphs. This is not a hard real-time solver or a distributed cache.

The input loader rejects duplicate keys and nonfinite JSON, bounds file bytes, collection sizes and expression depth, and validates cycles/dangling references before evaluation. The SDK accepts validated manifests; constructing internal dataclasses is unsupported. The test suite includes independent brute-force subset enumeration for random mixed AND/OR DAGs, temporal boundary equivalence, an external-conflict source, and atomic output interruption.

Fingerprints cover canonical protocol content and normalize order of sources/claims/answers and times. They do not sign origin. Support-expression order is retained as authored, so two logically equivalent formulas need not have the same fingerprint. Evaluation at the same manifest/instant is deterministic and offline.
